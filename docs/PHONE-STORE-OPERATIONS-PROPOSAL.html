Decision requestedApprove preparation for a one-branch pilot. Give operations and finance ownership of the handoffs from web order to invoice and from repair job to collection. Open the pilot once the reconciliation and access checks pass.

The current system covers catalog browsing, stock reservations, sales, purchasing and repair work. The next phase should make every completed transaction traceable to the correct stock movement, payment and daily report.

Source review of the local working tree at 867da6b plus local edits. The screen diagrams are schematics. Deployment, live data, role journeys and database test outcomes require staging evidence.

01 / Current system

Where work changes hands

The storefront and Arabic staff app share Supabase. Staff sign in through Supabase Auth. Checkout reaches the database through the protected order function in production.

01 · Web reservation

  1. Browse
  2. Reserve
  3. Confirm
  4. Ready
  5. Deliver
  6. Invoice + tender

Customer → storefront → order function → fulfilment staff. An unconfirmed hold can expire. Staff can cancel with a reason. Delivery updates stock and IMEI where required.

02 · Counter sale and return

  1. Open drawer
  2. Invoice + tender
  3. Stock / IMEI
  4. Close and count

Cashier posts the sale. High discounts can trigger approval. A return follows invoice → approval when required → refund → inspection → sellable or rejected disposition.

03 · Purchasing and payables

  1. Supplier
  2. Draft PO
  3. Approval
  4. Send + receive
  5. Supplier invoice
  6. Pay

Manager → owner approval under the default policy → receiving staff → accountant. Receipts can be partial. Tracked products require serials. Invoice mismatches or larger payments can require approval.

04 · Repair and parts

  1. Intake
  2. Diagnose + quote
  3. Customer decision
  4. Repair + test
  5. Hand back
  6. Payment + warranty

Cashier records custody and customer response. Technician quotes. If a part is missing, a manager approves and receives it before work proceeds. Declined or unrepairable jobs still need a recorded handback.

Dashed steps mark proposed handoffs. Stock, finance and custody records use different references today. The proposal connects them at the point each job is closed.

02 / Gap analysis

What to fix before expanding

P0 · Revenue completeness

Web fulfilment needs a financial posting

Delivery releases the reservation and records stock and device changes. The owner summary reads sales invoices, lines and tenders. A web delivery through the current route can therefore reduce stock without appearing in that sales total. Create a linked posting with a unique order reference, payment status and refund path.

Evidence · 0009_device_tracking.sql, set_online_order_status · 0007_sales_returns.sql, owner_summary

P0 · Inventory and branch boundaries

Define who owns an online reservation

Online stock checks aggregate by variant and delivery posts against the first active branch. The delivery function checks the staff role and selects in-stock serials by variant without a branch condition. Confirmed holds also have no finite expiry in code. For pilot, make one fulfilment branch explicit and test a second branch’s attempted access and concurrent reservations for the last unit.

Evidence · 0005_storefront_orders.sql, catalog and reservations · 0009_device_tracking.sql, set_online_order_status

P0 · Release evidence

Prove the protected checkout and staff access in staging

Turnstile, IP limiting and direct RPC lockdown exist in source. Gather evidence for deployment, valid and invalid checkout, staff roles, owner MFA, restore and operational alerting. Use synthetic fixtures in a separate staging project.

Evidence · RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md · docs/ORDER-ENDPOINT-SETUP.md · 0010_order_endpoint_lockdown.sql

P1 · Service profitability and customer proof

Close the repair record financially

Quotes, decisions, parts consumption and custody are recorded. The reviewed repair flow has no payment posting, warranty issuance or printable handover receipt. Link labour, parts cost, collection, receipt and warranty to the repair reference. Tie missing parts to purchase and supplier receipts.

Evidence · RepairOrderDialog.tsx · Maintenance.tsx · 0011–0014 repair migrations · docs/REPAIR-DASHBOARD-PLAN.md

P1 · Data consistency

Save catalog edits and PO drafts as single operations

Product and variant writes run separately. PO draft saving writes the header, deletes lines and reinserts lines in separate requests. A failed request can leave a partial change. Use transactional saves and test retries and edits from two sessions.

Evidence · apps/admin/src/pages/Products.tsx and PurchaseOrders.tsx

03 / Proposed delivery

Four gates to a wider rollout

Sequenced by dependency. Scope and timing can be estimated after the owner, manager and accountant confirm the operating rules.

Gate 0 · Owner + engineering

Establish staging

Apply migrations through 0014 in a separate project. Provision test users for all seven roles. Exercise migration tests, checkout controls, backup restore and alerts.

Exit: Attach results for all 10 SQL test scripts, the role-by-branch matrix and a completed restore drill.

Gate 1 · Operations + finance

Close the web order loop

Assign the fulfilment branch, hold rules and posting trigger. Link order, invoice, tender, stock/IMEI and returns. Add exceptions to the dashboard.

Exit: Deliver or cancel a staged order. A retry creates one posting and one stock deduction. A failed or cancelled order releases the hold as agreed.

Gate 2 · Service manager + finance

Close the repair loop

Add receipts, collection and warranty. Link part requests to procurement and reconcile service stock and charges.

Exit: Complete a normal repair, a declined quote and a missing-part repair. Each device has a custody trail and each charge or refund reconciles.

Gate 3 · Owner + branch manager

Run the branch pilot

Train role holders, set the opening balance and stock baseline, and rehearse closeout and rollback. Start with reserve-and-confirm.

Exit: Staff and finance sign off on consecutive daily closes against the agreed baseline. Resolve unexplained stock and cash differences before expansion.

Track during the pilotShare of delivered orders with a linked invoice and tender record, unexplained drawer differences, overdue confirmed holds, and repairs awaiting parts or handback. Establish baselines from the pilot records.

Later decisions from the original plan include payroll and commission rules, bank and wallet reconciliation, online payments and shipping integration, owner alerts or an app, marketing attribution and the legacy POS bridge. Confirm the POS system and source-of-truth boundaries before estimating integration.

04 / Owner workshop

Decisions needed for the pilot

Owner and manager · Pilot boundaries

Choose the branch, fulfilment owner, pickup and delivery rules, hold duration after confirmation and who resolves exceptions. Confirm whether an existing desktop POS remains the accounting source during the pilot.

Accountant · Posting and reconciliation

Define when to recognize web and repair revenue, who records tender, how returns and refunds post, and which records must match at daily close. Confirm costing, approval limits and tax treatment before reporting them.

Service manager · Quote and handback

Agree how customer approval is captured, when parts are reserved or consumed, what a receipt shows, and who owns warranty claims and devices still in custody.

Engineering · Evidence pack

Document the migration version, synthetic scenarios, role access results, restore evidence and a rollout checklist. Include reference IDs for reconciliation without exporting real customer data.
